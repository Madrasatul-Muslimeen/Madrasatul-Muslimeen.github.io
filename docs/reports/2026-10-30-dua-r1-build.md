What each kind of Dua opens:
- **A Dua from the Hadith books** (Rasulullah, Hadith Books): Hadith Study's own Dua card (`duaCard` in
  `dua-browser.js`, unchanged), with its progress row, notes, bookmark and ✓ Check. A narration's reference opens that
  book in `hadith-collections.html` with ← Back; a word's Word card comes back through `?view=dua&dua=<n>`.
- **A Hisn al-Muslim dhikr** (Occasions): its chapter, words in the Qur'anic font, how many times, al-Qaḥṭānī's
  reference, and, where tied, "The same dua in the Hadith books ▸ Open Dua n" (matched by the computer, to be checked),
  with ← Back to the very dhikr.
- **A Prophet's dua** (21, from the Qur'an): who, a short title, the āyah, its meaning, and "Open in Quran Study ↗"
  (`quranrevival.html?goto=S:A&back=1`).
- **Sahaba, Salaf, Ulama**: empty, each saying in words why (a checked source is needed; J104 tick 6).

Where the reader is (group, part, list page, open Dua, an opened tie) is kept in the page's address, so every way back
from another page lands exactly there (decision 86).

## The data

`tools/hadith-data-pull/dua-groups.mjs` (new) writes `tools/hadith-data-pull/output/dua/groups.json` (526 KB, 97 KB
gzipped) from data already in the repository: the 3,126 Dua cards and their vowels, Hisn al-Muslim
(`sources/hisn-asellam.json`, asellam/HisnElMuslim, MIT, credited in the file) and the Qur'an data. Deterministic (two
runs, one checksum). No existing file under `output/dua/` was moved or renamed.
- Occasions: Hisn al-Muslim's 133 chapters in 15 occasions, 302 adhkār. A dhikr is tied to a Dua card only where
  `dua-vowels.mjs` found the card's whole dua in it word for word: 78 ties, each shown "to be checked".
- Hadith Books: every dua narrated in each book (not only those it anchors), most narrated first, with the book's
  standard number. The group's count counts each dua once (3,126).
- A list line is the dua's first eight words (vowelled where a source holds them); where no words are picked out yet
  (2,035 of 3,126), the book's chapter heading stands in, in a quieter style.
