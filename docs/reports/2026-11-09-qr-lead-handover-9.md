## Lessons this session
- Do not write `cat > $TMPDIR/x` (empty variable) in a command: it waits on stdin and hangs.
- Quran data is in `tools/quran-data-pull/output/surahs/surah_NNN.json`.
- A pause list is `[from,to]` pairs; `layOut()` needs `{from,to,mid}`.
- Do not claim a feature exists in a PMS comment before it is built.
- PMS writes: fetch with `out_dir`, edit with Python, write back with `file_path` and `if_version`.
