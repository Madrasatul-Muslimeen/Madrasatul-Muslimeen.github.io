## Lessons this session
- **PMS writes (ArtifactData): the document's fields are TOP-LEVEL.** `{"data":{...}}` creates a stray `data` field (I did this twice and removed both with `{"data":{"__delete__":true}}`). To append to an array (needs, comments) get the doc with `out_dir`, edit the JSON in Python, write the whole file back with `file_path`, pinned with `if_version`.
- The artifact page's CSP blocks audio from other sites: demos embed audio as data: URIs.
- Do not guess a cut you cannot hear: look at the real numbers (loudness per 10 ms, pitch, zero-crossing rate) first, and give the Owner a way to move it.
- A PR can merge while you push: after a merge notice check `git merge-base --is-ancestor <head> origin/main`.
- Installing speech models/packages was blocked by the sandbox classifier; do not work around it.
- Playwright from the repo: `ln -sfn /opt/node-tools/node_modules node_modules` in the folder of the script, run, remove.
