## Lessons this session
- **PCF writes with `ArtifactData update`: pass the document's fields AT THE TOP LEVEL of `data` (`{needs: [...], act: {...}, updated: "..."}`), never wrapped in `{data: {...}}`.** A `get` shows the fields inside `"data":{...}`; I wrapped them once and had to delete the nested key (`{"data": {"__delete__": true}}`). Pin every write with `if_version`, re-`get` to check it reads clean.
- A mutation that "changes nothing the page sees" is UNPROVEN, not green: `rowopen` (rank 1 is the same word on both lists) and `startson` (the card resets the switch anyway) were both unproven; fix the check or drop the mutation, never leave it green.
- A check with `A || B` that can pass either way is a cannot-fail check: I wrote one ("next word starts OFF") and tightened it before running mutations.
- A suite must FAIL under a mutation, not crash or hang: tolerant clicks (`tap()` with a short timeout and catch), null-safe reads (`?.`), return at once when a control is absent (a 12s wait per call across 7 viewports looked like a hang), and a `finally` that closes the browser.
- Before building on a persisted setting, grep who reads it: `banglaState.on` fed the Wheel and Learn next as well as the card.
- `node serve.js` dies when the MCP tools reconnect: a browser suite that reports `ECONNREFUSED` or "navigating to localhost:8080" means restart it (`(nohup node serve.js >/dev/null 2>&1 &)`), and `tools/i18n-verify/node_modules` must be symlinked to `/opt/node-tools/node_modules`.
- The ambiguous words in a job (J134 "Words also in Bangla" switch; J132 "Study ▾ goes away") are cheapest to settle by reading the PCF job text, then ticks in the job's Needs, then carry on with the rest.
- Do not read old transcripts. Hand over at about 30% context used (check `get_session` `external_metadata.context_usage`).
