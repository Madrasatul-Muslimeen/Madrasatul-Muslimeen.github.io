## What was built

- **D22** the Dua Card's menu. On the card only the Dua itself (its words, the word panel, the vowels switch) and the
  progress block stay open; the progress block keeps its My Notes and Bookmark buttons, which are part of it. A menu
  between the words and the progress opens each other block as a pop-up card with ✕ Close back to the Dua:
  **Meaning** (the HadeethEnc and hadith-api translations, the first one unfolded; "No meaning for this dua in the app
  yet." when there is none), **Source** (the book and number, "Narrated in n places" and every narration button),
  **The whole narration**, and **✓ Check** (Owner and Prime only, as before; the pop-up closes and the check panel opens
  under the card). Esc, a tap outside and ✕ all close it, and focus goes back to the menu button. The blocks are moved,
  not copied, so their own buttons, folds and loads behave exactly as before. Hisn al-Muslim cards: Source holds
  al-Qaḥṭānī's reference, the tied Dua card and the credit. Prophets' cards: Meaning holds the āyah's meaning, Source
  the "Open in Quran Study ↗" link.
  `duaCard()` in `dua-browser.js` is **not changed**: the re-layout is done in `dua-study.js` around it, so Hadith
  Study's own Dua tab is the same (`dua-tab-browser` 63/0).
- **D12** the top banner frame: a green arch with "بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ" and "Top banner · text to come", above every card.
- **D11** the bottom banner, cut from the Owner's Asma ul Husna poster template
  (`docs/reference/2026-10-07-asma-poster-template-al-witr.png`, box 48,1212–1008,1450, as `prep2.py` did) into the
  Dua-owned file `app/img/dua-banner-bottom.webp` (40 KB), under every card.
- **D05** titles, English and Bangla, in `groups.json` (`titles`, made by `dua-groups.mjs`) until D03 can save them.
  Shown on the card (above it, in the frame) and on its list row. Where they come from, honestly: **the 21 Prophets'
  duas** have titles in both languages (already in R1); **44 of the 3,126 Hadith-book duas** take their HadeethEnc
  hadith title, the only short titles the repository holds (English ≤ 90 characters, Bangla ≤ 110, quotation marks and
  end stops removed; 34 of them have Bangla too). They are hadith titles, not titles written for the dua, so some read
  as the hadith's story. **Every other dua shows its book's chapter heading in Arabic** (the Hisn dhikr its chapter),
  and the rest only their number. Titles for all of them need D03 (the Owner writes and saves them), which waits on
  J104 tick 9 (the Rules change). No Hisn dhikr gained an English title: none of the 78 tied cards is among the 44.
