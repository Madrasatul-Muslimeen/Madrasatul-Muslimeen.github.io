Tools for Al-Hadid 1–6

The 1 on ⋯ is your note count, so you still see at a glance that this āyah has a note.

One Read bar, a Meaning button, and the three study buttons at the bottom

  1. Meaning (📖) switches the page between the Mushaf and a reading flow. The flow shows every āyah of the page with its Arabic, English, Bangla and word-by-word meanings, and it carries on to the next page as you scroll. Pressing it again brings the Mushaf back at the same āyah.
  2. In full screen (⤢), only the reference, 📖 and ⤢ stay on the bar, as in your screenshot. So you can switch between Mushaf and Meaning without leaving full screen.
  3. Text size, Bookmark, Notes, Mark complete, Writing sheet and Attach to Asma go under one ⋯ button. Two rows of buttons become one.
  4. Take an Approach · Record Your Progress · Know Your Status move to one slim row just above the four bottom buttons, where a thumb reaches them.
  5. Prime sits beside Search, as one word, so it no longer takes a row.
Try: tap an āyah on the page, then 📖 Meaning (it opens at that āyah). Untick a layer at the top. Tap ⤢ for full screen, then 📖 again. Open ⋯.
Saved: on a phone the reading starts about 150 pixels higher, about four lines of Mushaf. On a computer the bar has room, so ⋯ shows its tools in a line instead.
