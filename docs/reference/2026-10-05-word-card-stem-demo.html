Make the main part of a word stand out

The main part of a word is its stem: the verb or noun itself, without the little pieces in front (like بِ "by" and ال "the") or at the end. Each option marks it the same way everywhere on the card: in the big word at the top, in the English meaning, in the Stem box and in the Dictionary word box (on WbW and Basic). Try each one in both looks and on both words, then tell me A, B or C, or "keep today's".
