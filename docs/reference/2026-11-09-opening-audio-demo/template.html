Honest limits. The line-by-line timing is exact, because it comes from the audio itself. The word-by-word light is an estimate: each word gets time in proportion to its letters, between the first sound and the end of the line. Exact word times need a hand check, like the sūrah timing helper. The cut points are found from the quiet moments in your recording; if one is off, move it with the buttons and play again. The audio is not saved anywhere. For the app, the two short pieces would be saved as small files from your recording, which is your decision to make (hosting).
