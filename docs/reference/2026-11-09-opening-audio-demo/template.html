Opening Recitation Demo

The app's opening card, with the recitation playing. Each line appears when its recitation starts, and the next line comes in when the recitation ends. Nothing is timed by guess: the screen follows the audio.

A'udhu billahi min ash-shaytwaner Rajeem
I seek refuge with Allah from shaitwan, the rejected, accursed.
In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful.

Honest limits. The line-by-line timing is exact, because it comes from the audio itself. The word-by-word light is an estimate: each word gets time in proportion to its letters, between the first sound and the end of the line. Exact word times need a hand check, like the sūrah timing helper. The recitations in this demo are free files from everyayah.com, used here only to show the idea; for the app I would play them from that site, not copy them, and the Owner can choose another voice.
