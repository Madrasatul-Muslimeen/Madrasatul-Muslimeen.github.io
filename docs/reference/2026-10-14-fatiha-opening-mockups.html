Aʿūdhu billāh and Bismillah before Al-Fātiḥah

Your answers in PCF J26 (14 Oct): yes to a new permanent key for Aʿūdhu billāh, and both shown separately, not counted in Al-Fātiḥah's 7 āyāt. Here are three places they can sit on Al-Fātiḥah's Read page, at phone width.

Your choice: tick A, B or C in PCF J26, or mix them (for example “A, and also in the picker like C”). In all three, tapping Aʿūdhu billāh or Bismillah opens the same card an āyah opens: the Approaches, Record Your Progress with 👥, notes and bookmark, with ← Back to where you were.
