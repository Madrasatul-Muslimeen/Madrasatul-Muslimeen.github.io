Dua wheel — DEMO v1.08 sample data

For the Owner's tick (PCF MSA J104 item 19). Nothing here is saved; the numbers are invented to show the shapes. Four things: 1 the Dua wheel with its layers, 2 marking a dua like a bookmark, 3 the Dua Word Card's level wheel (same as the Qur'an Word Card), 4 a wheel for one whole dua, one segment per word.

1 · The Dua wheel (each slice of the circle is one dua; each ring groups the same slices a different way)

Tap a coloured arc on any ring (or a row below) to see its status: how many duas Not started, Learning, Practising, Achieved, Mastered.

2 · Marking a dua, like making a bookmark

Press the ribbon on a dua. You choose its status and its own category. A category I placed for you carries a small “placed by Claude” mark; change it any time as you study, and it becomes yours.

3 · Dua Word Card — the levels wheel (same as the Qur'an Word Card)

بِاسْمِكَ

Tap a level to mark it. WbW · Root · Family · Taṣrīf · Depth.

A word that is also in the Qur'an goes to the Qur'an wheel as well (the same word, one record). Words found only in duas fill this wheel only.

4 · One dua as a wheel — one segment per word

Each segment is a word, coloured by how well you know it. Tap a word to cycle its status.
