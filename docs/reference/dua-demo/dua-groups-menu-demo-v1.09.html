Dua groups and Menu: DEMO v1.09 sample data

For the Owner's tick (PCF MSA J104 item 25). Nothing here is saved; the groups are invented. Three parts: 1 add, rename, move and delete groups, with sub groups up to 3 levels; 2 a group opening in a pop-up you can drag and resize (full screen on a phone); 3 two Menu layouts to choose from. Try it on your phone, tablet and computer.

1 · Groups and sub groups

Up to 3 levels. ↑ ↓ move within the group; ⇥ puts a group inside the one above; ⇤ brings it out one level. Delete asks first.

2 · A group as a pop-up

Press Open on any group above. On a computer or tablet: drag the title bar to move it, drag the corner (bottom right) to resize. On a phone it fills the screen.

3 · Menu layouts (the same buttons, two arrangements)

Both keep the same four heads: Share, Keep, Listen, Study. Narrow the window to see the phone shape, widen it to see tablet and computer.

Layout A: all four rows, tidied

Phone: four rows, two buttons across. Tablet and computer: two columns (Share and Keep on the left, Listen and Study on the right), so the Menu is about half as tall.

Layout B: one head at a time

Phone and tablet: four tabs, only the chosen head's buttons show (the shortest Menu). Computer (wide): all four side by side in one row, no tabs.
