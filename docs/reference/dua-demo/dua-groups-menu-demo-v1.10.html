Nothing here is saved; the groups are invented. 1 groups you drag to any level, one menu per group, Admin's groups or My groups; 2 a group pop-up (full screen on tablet and phone; on a computer drag it and resize it from any side or corner); 3 Menu layout B with five tabs in one row.

1 · Groups: drag to any level

Press and hold the ⠿ handle, then drag. Drop on the top edge of a group to go before it, the bottom edge to go after it, the middle to go inside it. Up to 3 levels. Tap a group's name to open it; tap ⋯ for its menu (Open, Rename, Add inside, Delete). Works with a mouse or a finger.

2 · A group as a pop-up

Tablet and phone: full screen. Computer (a wide screen with a mouse): drag the title bar to move; drag any of the four sides or four corners to resize.

Open a group above.
