What would change in the app

  1. The Read bar gets one Notes button. It opens this pane on the āyah you are reading.
  2. The pane is the Mapping My Journey note editor: folders, versions, tags and the full toolbar. A note written here is in your folders straight away.
  3. It lists every note started on this āyah or mentioning it, with + New note and Open in Mapping My Journey.
  4. Track this āyah becomes a tab of the pane. QCR stays on the Āyah card and the ⋮ menu.
  5. The separate Note view retires. Old bookmarks that opened it open Read with the pane instead.
  6. Notes you already wrote in the Note view are copied into your folders, only if you say yes. Nothing is deleted.
