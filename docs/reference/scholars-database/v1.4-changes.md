# Scholars Database v1.4: what changed

The Owner's request (PCF J02, 12 Oct 2026): *"Add the scholars Birth-death both timing, Political Era, Madhab
attachement, etc. for all tafsir scholars"*, with decision 108 Q3 (*a work sits in the era the BOOK was written*).
Built from v1.3 (which was checked identical to the live page before starting).

**Every value Claude added or changed is a draft.** On Claude's 48 rows the whole row was already a draft
(`"draft": true`), so the changes sit inside it. On the Owner's 20 rows **no Owner value was touched** (checked field
by field by the build): the new book dates went into column I, *Qurʾān works*, which is Claude's and highlighted as a
draft (`draftNew`). Each ✓ Checked clears them. The page code is unchanged; only its data changed.

## Counts

| Field | Rows | What happened |
|---|---|---|
| Birth and death in AH **and** CE (`lifeAH`, `lifeCE`) | 68 of 68 carry both | Already present for every row. 1 birth year filled (al-Māturīdī), 1 death year corrected (Ismāʿīl Ḥaqqī), 2 disagreements noted (al-Rāzī, Ibn Kathīr). 8 birth years stay unknown (below). |
| Political authority / era (`authority`) | 68 of 68 | Already present from v1.3, still drafts. Unchanged. |
| Madhab (`madhab`, column H) | 68 of 68 | Already present from v1.2, unchanged. No row predates the madhabs: the earliest rows are the founders themselves (Abū Ḥanīfah, Mālik), so "before the madhabs" was not needed anywhere. |
| Written date of each book (new `books` list, shown in column F on Claude's rows and column I on the Owner's) | 68 of 68 | 137 books: **136 dated, 1 empty**. 11 dates are firm (the author's own note, or a well-known account); 125 are approximate ("c.") or estimates ("est."), and 83 of those are only the death year ("est. before … from the death year"). |

Each book in `books` carries `title`, `written` (the text shown), `yearAH` (the year used to place the book in an era;
for an estimate, the death year or the end of the range), `estimate`, `by: "Claude"`, `draft: true`.

## Lifespan changes, row by row

- **s023 al-Māturīdī**: birth filled as **c. 238 AH / c. 853 CE**, an estimate (no date in the early sources; some say
  c. 256 AH / c. 870 CE).
- **s056 Ismāʿīl Ḥaqqī al-Bursawī**: death corrected from 1127 AH / 1715 CE to **1137 AH / 1725 CE**, the date commonly
  given. v1.3 had it wrong (it was Claude's own draft).
- **s038 Fakhr al-Dīn al-Rāzī**: birth 544 AH kept, "some say 543" added.
- **s048 Ibn Kathīr**: birth c. 700 AH kept, "some say 701" added.

## Left empty, and why

**Birth year, 8 rows** (shown as "?" as before): the sources give no birth year, and no commonly cited value exists to
mark "c.". s024 Abū al-Layth al-Samarqandī; s025 al-Thaʿlabī; s030 al-Wāḥidī; s040 al-Qurṭubī (modern estimates of
"c. 600 AH" exist but are not a cited date); s041 al-Bayḍāwī; s042 al-Nasafī; s051 Ibn ʿĀdil; s058 Sulaymān al-Jamal.

**Book date, 1 book**: s020 Shuʿayb al-Arnāʾūṭ, *Tahqīq of Sharḥ Maʿānī al-Āthār* (the Owner's title). The Arnāʾūṭ
edition commonly known is of al-Ṭaḥāwī's *Sharḥ Mushkil al-Āthār* (1415 AH / 1994 CE); whether the Owner means that
book needs the Owner's word, so no date was given.

## Dates the Owner should look at first

These are the approximate dates most likely to be argued over: al-Bukhārī's Ṣaḥīḥ (c. 217–232 AH), Muslim's Ṣaḥīḥ
(c. 235–250 AH), al-Ṭabrisī's Majmaʿ al-Bayān (c. 530–536 AH), al-Khāzin (c. 725 AH), Abū al-Suʿūd (c. 973 AH),
Ismāʿīl Ḥaqqī's Rūḥ al-Bayān (c. 1117 AH), Shāh Walī Allāh's Fatḥ al-Raḥmān (c. 1151 AH), al-Shawkānī's two books, al-Saʿdī
(c. 1344 AH), and al-Albānī's and al-Arnāʾūṭ's publication years. As the README says, check them against a biographical
dictionary (al-Dhahabī's Siyar, al-Ziriklī's al-Aʿlām) before the app relies on them.

## Checking

`python3 docs/reference/scholars-database/check-page-data.py v1.4` (from the repository root) checks that the JSON
parses, that the page's embedded data equals the JSON file, and that the page is the template with only the data
replaced. It passes for v1.3 and v1.4, and fails when one character of the page's data is changed.
