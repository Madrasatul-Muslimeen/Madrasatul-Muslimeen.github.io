The rules, in short

  • Each Arabic letter has one Bangla letter: ب ব, ث and ص ছ, ذ ز ظ য, ح and ه হ, ض দ, ط ত, ق and ك ক, ع ‘ (as in না‘বুদু).
  • Short vowels: fatḥa া, kasra ি, ḍamma ু. A long ā is া- (as in মা-লিকি); long ī and ū are ী and ূ.
  • A doubled letter (shadda) is written twice: রাব্বি. The sun letters take the l: আর্রাহমা-নি, আদ্দীনি.
  • Each word is read on its own, so ٱلْ at the start is আল and ٱهْدِنَا is ইহদিনা-. The name Allah is always আল্লা-হ.
  • Letters the muṣḥaf marks as silent are left out (the alif in آمَنُوا۟).
