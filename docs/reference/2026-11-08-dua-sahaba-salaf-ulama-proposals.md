## Left out on purpose
- Duas a Companion asked the Prophet Sallallahu A'laihi Wa Sallam to teach (for example Abū Bakr's morning-and-evening words): they are the Prophet's, and already in his group.
- Where the Companion only repeats what the Prophet Sallallahu A'laihi Wa Sallam said: also his group.
