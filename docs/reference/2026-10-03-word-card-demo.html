From the data This āyah uses three forms of the root side by side:
يُعَلِّمُونَForm II · teachto make others know

Grammar rule Form V (تَفَعَّلَ) is the "self" side of Form II: what Form II does to others, Form V takes on oneself, often gradually or with effort. (Fixed text for each form; you review it once.)
Needs a source Distinctions between near-synonyms, e.g. عِلْم and مَعْرِفَة, need a recognised book such as al-ʿAskarī's al-Furūq al-Lughawiyya or al-Rāghib's al-Mufradāt.
