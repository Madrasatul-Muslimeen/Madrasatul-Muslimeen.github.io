What gets saved

  • Everyone on the roster, their roles and who looks after whom
  • Every claim and confirmation, for each person you can record for
  • Every āyah note, with its formatting
  • Every bookmark and folder
  • The whole activity log, week by week
  • The subject tree, the Approaches, ladders, levels and domains
  • Āyah collections (QCR) and Asma ul Husna collections
  • Classes, course offers, enrolments and curriculum
  • Homework and submissions, and your own teaching notes
  • This madrasah's own settings, banner and taglines

Not saved, because it is not yours to lose: the Qur'an text, the Mushaf pages, the recitations and the app's own program code. Those are the same for everyone and are kept separately.

Where to keep it. Save it somewhere that is not this computer as well — a phone, a cloud drive, an email to yourself. A backup on the same machine as the thing it backs up is only half a backup. Making a fresh one takes a few seconds, so do it before anything unusual: a big change, a new person, a term ending.

Reading it later. Double-click the file. It opens as an ordinary page with everything laid out and a filter box at the top. Nothing about it needs this app to still exist.
