## How to read this

Every line below is **one node**. Nodes are all the same shape. Whether a node
is a "subject" or a "domain" is not a property it owns — it is decided by how
it is used:

- A node with children beneath it acts as a **domain**.
- A node with nothing beneath it acts as a **subject**.
- Any node can also be **pointed at** by a record as its theme — which is the
  second way a node acts as a domain (see Part 4).

Nothing needs converting. A node changes role by gaining a child, losing a
child, or being pointed at.

  (`Life Skill (Trading)` → Life Skill ▸ Trading)

  (`Aqhlaq (Behavior)` → Akhlaq, gloss "Behaviour")

## PART 4 — Domains on records

A **record** — one thing actually taught — may point at any number of nodes as
its theme. This is what carries your teaching method: teach the Quran first,
then note what the teaching turned out to be about.

```
subject: Quran    unit: ayah:2:282           domains: [Sharia]
subject: Hadith   unit: hadith:bukhari:5678  domains: [Health Study]
subject: Quran    unit: ayah:4:11            domains: [Sharia, Fiqh, Akhlaq]
```

Any node in Part 1 may serve as a domain. Nodes most likely to be used this
way: Sharia, Fiqh, Aqeedah, Ebadah, Akhlaq, Ethics, Health Study, Al-Ghayb,
Islamic History & Story, Nature Studies, Life Skill, Islamic Mindset,
Islamic Lifestyle.

This list is not fixed and nothing needs pre-declaring — any node becomes a
domain the moment a record points at it.
